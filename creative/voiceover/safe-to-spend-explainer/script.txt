How much money is safe to spend after your bills?
Open the account you pay bills from and write down the available balance, not the current one.
Write down your next payday, then list every bill, subscription and card payment that leaves before it.
Subtract them.
Subtract what you're holding back: tax money, a deposit, the cushion you don't want to touch.
Write the result at the top of a note and date it.
