## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Facebook Reel, 1080×1920, 12–26s, hook by 1.5s, sound off | An older, sound-off-leaning audience: every point on screen, held long enough to read, plainly worded. |
| **Product** | Home Base: $49 (list $99), motif "tag", calm personality, cool accent #4f7a5c | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You own or rent a home and would rather be told what needs you this week than keep a mental list you know is incomplete. / You want the boring, expensive things caught early: the water heater, the gutters, the outside tap before the first freeze. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | What it is not: Earn trust by stating what the product deliberately does not do, then show what it does instead. | Best fit for a conversion film on Facebook Reel (platform fit 1, goal fit 3). Other candidates: One screen, closely (-1.4), In their words (-1.6). |
| **Angle** | "You manage more than one property." | A trust film: the listing's own boundaries, then the thing it does instead, then how it treats your data. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "tag" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "tag": things in the home are tagged, so scenes rise like a tag being lifted. |
| **Camera** | Camera "dolly" | Camera "dolly": constant forward movement for a high-energy feed. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → light → ink → accent | Grounds light → light → ink → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, mid-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 20.1s, 5 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Facebook Reel (0.3s a word), inside 12–26s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `dolly`, motif `tag`, transitions `slideUp` → `cardSlide` → `slideUp`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
