# home-management-companion--facebook-feed--consideration

**Facebook feed video** · consideration · 1080×1350 · 18.5s · structure: **whatYouGet**

> Angle: "What you get" — `micro`

## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Facebook feed video, 1080×1350, 8–20s, hook by 1.5s, sound off | Scrolled past in a feed with sound off: say who it is for in the first seconds, name the product early, keep every point on screen and readable at a glance. |
| **Product** | Home Base: $49 (list $99), motif "tag", calm personality, cool accent #4f7a5c | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You own or rent a home and would rather be told what needs you this week than keep a mental list you know is incomplete. / You want the boring, expensive things caught early: the water heater, the gutters, the outside tap before the first freeze. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | What you get: Open on the product, then show what is in it, then the price. | Best fit for a consideration film on Facebook feed video (platform fit 2, goal fit 1). Other candidates: Problem cascade (2.0), What it is not (1.9). |
| **Angle** | "What you get" | A conversion film for people who already know the problem: product first, contents, price. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "tag" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "tag": things in the home are tagged, so scenes rise like a tag being lifted. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → light → ink → accent | Grounds light → light → ink → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Edit** | Trimmed "contents" to 3 items | Fewer items read properly beats more items skimmed. |
| **Edit** | Trimmed "contents" to 2 items | Fewer items read properly beats more items skimmed. |
| **Edit** | Cut "brand" | It would run past 20s, the most Facebook feed video holds attention for; the end card still names the product. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 18.5s, 3 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Facebook feed video (0.32s a word), inside 8–20s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `locked`, motif `tag`, transitions `slideUp` → `cardSlide` → `slideUp`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
