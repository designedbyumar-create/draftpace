# home-management-companion--instagram-reel--conversion

**Instagram Reel** · conversion · 1080×1920 · 19.6s · structure: **walkthrough**

## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Instagram Reel, 1080×1920, 12–26s, hook by 1.2s, sound on | Full-screen and swipeable: the first second decides whether anyone stays. Mostly watched with sound, but captions still carry every point. The right edge holds like/comment buttons and the bottom holds the caption, so text lives in the middle band. |
| **Product** | Home Base: $49 (list $99), motif "tag", calm personality, cool accent #4f7a5c | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You own or rent a home and would rather be told what needs you this week than keep a mental list you know is incomplete. / You want the boring, expensive things caught early: the water heater, the gutters, the outside tap before the first freeze. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | How it works: Three real steps, each on the real screen where it happens. | Best fit for a conversion film on Instagram Reel (platform fit 2, goal fit 1). Other candidates: Problem cascade (4.0), What it is not (3.5). |
| **Angle** | "How it works" | The listing's own how-it-works, shortest three steps in their real order. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "tag" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "tag": things in the home are tagged, so scenes rise like a tag being lifted. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → accent → light → ink | Grounds light → accent → light → ink: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Full sound design, mid-energy bed | Sound-on placement: every transition, landing and reveal has a cue, and the bed ducks under the brand moment. |
| **Length** | 19.6s, 5 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Instagram Reel (0.26s a word), inside 12–26s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `tag`, transitions `slideUp` → `cardSlide` → `slideUp`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
