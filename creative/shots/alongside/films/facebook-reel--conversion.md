# alongside--facebook-reel--conversion

> Angle: "You put something down half finished and lose where you were." — `problemsSolved[2].problem`

## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Facebook Reel, 1080×1920, 12–26s, hook by 1.5s, sound off | An older, sound-off-leaning audience: every point on screen, held long enough to read, plainly worded. |
| **Product** | ADHD Life Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "focus", calm personality, warm accent #86586a | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You have ADHD, or executive function that works like it does, and most productivity tools have made that worse rather than better. / You are dealing with brain fog from long covid, a concussion, chronic illness, grief, new parenthood, menopause, or depression, and holding a plan in your head has stopped being reliable. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | Problem cascade: Open on one real problem, let the related worries pile up, then cut to the product holding it. | Best fit for a conversion film on Facebook Reel (platform fit 2, goal fit 2). Other candidates: One screen, closely (-1.4), What it is not (-3.3). |
| **Angle** | "You put something down half finished and lose where you were." | Problem 3 of the listing, "You put something down half finished and lose where you were.", answered by its own solution. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "focus" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "focus": it is about one thing at a time, so scenes open like an iris onto one thing. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → ink → light → accent | Grounds light → ink → light → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, mid-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 23.7s, 5 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Facebook Reel (0.3s a word), inside 12–26s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `focus`, transitions `iris` → `blurDissolve` → `iris`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
