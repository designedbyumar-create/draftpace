## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Instagram Reel, 1080×1920, 12–26s, hook by 1.2s, sound on | Full-screen and swipeable: the first second decides whether anyone stays. Mostly watched with sound, but captions still carry every point. The right edge holds like/comment buttons and the bottom holds the caption, so text lives in the middle band. |
| **Product** | ADHD Life Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "focus", calm personality, warm accent #86586a | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You have ADHD, or executive function that works like it does, and most productivity tools have made that worse rather than better. / You are dealing with brain fog from long covid, a concussion, chronic illness, grief, new parenthood, menopause, or depression, and holding a plan in your head has stopped being reliable. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | Before and after: The problem told as the listing tells it, in the dark; then the product, in the light. | Best fit for a conversion film on Instagram Reel (platform fit 2, goal fit 2). Other candidates: How it works (4.0), Problem cascade (3.9). |
| **Angle** | "Most of what does not get done is not forgotten." | A sentence of the listing's own problem story, then the product answering it. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "focus" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "focus": it is about one thing at a time, so scenes open like an iris onto one thing. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds ink → light → accent → accent | Grounds ink → light → accent → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Sound** | Full sound design, mid-energy bed | Sound-on placement: every transition, landing and reveal has a cue, and the bed ducks under the brand moment. |
| **Length** | 12.0s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Instagram Reel (0.26s a word), inside 12–26s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `focus`, transitions `iris` → `blurDissolve` → `iris`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
