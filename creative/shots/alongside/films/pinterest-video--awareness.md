## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 6–15s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | ADHD Life Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "focus", calm personality, warm accent #86586a | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You have ADHD, or executive function that works like it does, and most productivity tools have made that worse rather than better. / You are dealing with brain fog from long covid, a concussion, chronic illness, grief, new parenthood, menopause, or depression, and holding a plan in your head has stopped being reliable. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | The question before buying: Answer the question people actually ask before they decide, with the listing's own answer. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 2, goal fit 0). Passed over: In their words (its real copy needs 17s to read), too long for 15s. Other candidates: In their words (7.7), How it works (2.7). |
| **Angle** | "Tried a to-do list and it just became another list to feel behind on?" | A deciding-stage question from the listing, "Tried a to-do list and it just became another list to feel behind on?", and its own answer. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "focus" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "focus": it is about one thing at a time, so scenes open like an iris onto one thing. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → light → accent → light | Grounds light → light → accent → light: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 14.7s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 6–15s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `focus`, transitions `iris` → `blurDissolve` → `iris`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
