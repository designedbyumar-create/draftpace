## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Instagram feed video, 1080×1350, 8–20s, hook by 1.2s, sound off | A 4:5 card in the grid of a feed, autoplayed muted. Craft and composition carry it; text is short and sits well inside the frame. |
| **Product** | ADHD Life Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "focus", calm personality, warm accent #86586a | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You have ADHD, or executive function that works like it does, and most productivity tools have made that worse rather than better. / You are dealing with brain fog from long covid, a concussion, chronic illness, grief, new parenthood, menopause, or depression, and holding a plan in your head has stopped being reliable. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | One screen, closely: Stay with a single real screen and read it slowly, the way someone planning would. | Best fit for a consideration film on Instagram feed video (platform fit 3, goal fit 2). Other candidates: How it works (-2.3), In their words (-2.7). |
| **Angle** | "Put something down so it comes back when it matters" | A slow read of "Everything you are holding": Everything being held, sorted into something to do, waiting on someone, something ongoing, each with Do this with me. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "focus" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "focus": it is about one thing at a time, so scenes open like an iris onto one thing. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → ink → light → accent | Grounds light → ink → light → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 12.1s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Instagram feed video (0.3s a word), inside 8–20s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `locked`, motif `focus`, transitions `iris` → `blurDissolve` → `iris`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
