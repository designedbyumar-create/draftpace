## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Instagram feed video, 1080×1350, 8–20s, hook by 1.2s, sound off | A 4:5 card in the grid of a feed, autoplayed muted. Craft and composition carry it; text is short and sits well inside the frame. |
| **Product** | Monthly Money Reset: free, motif "ledger", calm personality, cool accent #214b3e | From its definition and Shop listing. 1 real screens and live components (safeToSpendCard, nextActionCard) to show. |
| **Audience** | You want one clear number for what's actually safe to spend, not a full budget system. / You have a mix of bills, some paid automatically and some not, that you'd rather not accidentally spend past. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Material** | Allowed real lines up to 4 words longer | Every structure Monthly Money Reset's copy supports at Instagram feed video's normal line length is already used by its other films; a longer real line beats telling the same story twice. |
| **Structure** | Before and after: The problem told as the listing tells it, in the dark; then the product, in the light. | Best fit for a consideration film on Instagram feed video (platform fit 2, goal fit 0). Other candidates: Problem cascade (0.6), How it works (-2.2). |
| **Angle** | "It's hard to know, at a glance, what's actually free to spend without doing the math yourself every time." | A sentence of the listing's own problem story, then the product answering it. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "ledger" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "ledger": a ledger is ruled lines, so scenes are revealed row by row. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds ink → light → accent → accent | Grounds ink → light → accent → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 18.6s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Instagram feed video (0.3s a word), inside 8–20s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `locked`, motif `ledger`, transitions `lineWipe` → `blurDissolve` → `lineWipe`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
