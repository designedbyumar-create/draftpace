## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | TikTok, 1080×1920, 10–22s, hook by 0.8s, sound on | Native, fast and plain-spoken; polish that looks like an ad gets skipped. Sound-on. Hook inside a second. The deepest bottom and right overlays of any placement. |
| **Product** | Monthly Money Reset: free, motif "ledger", calm personality, cool accent #214b3e | From its definition and Shop listing. 1 real screens and live components (safeToSpendCard, nextActionCard) to show. |
| **Audience** | You want one clear number for what's actually safe to spend, not a full budget system. / You have a mix of bills, some paid automatically and some not, that you'd rather not accidentally spend past. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | What it is not: Earn trust by stating what the product deliberately does not do, then show what it does instead. | Best fit for a awareness film on TikTok (platform fit 2, goal fit 0). Other candidates: How it works (0.1), The question before buying (-1.0). |
| **Angle** | "You want automatic bank syncing or transaction import." | A trust film: the listing's own boundaries, then the thing it does instead, then how it treats your data. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "ledger" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "ledger": a ledger is ruled lines, so scenes are revealed row by row. TikTok wants native editing, so hard cuts and whips lead. |
| **Camera** | Camera "dolly" | Camera "dolly": constant forward movement for a high-energy feed. |
| **Colour** | Grounds ink → light → light → accent | Grounds ink → light → light → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Full sound design, high-energy bed | Sound-on placement: every transition, landing and reveal has a cue, and the bed ducks under the brand moment. |
| **Length** | 18.7s, 5 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on TikTok (0.24s a word), inside 10–22s. |

Treatment: voice `native`, camera `dolly`, motif `ledger`, transitions `cut` → `whip` → `lineWipe`, music bed at 0.24 (high energy).
