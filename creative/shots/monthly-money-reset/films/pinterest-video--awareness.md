# monthly-money-reset--pinterest-video--awareness

**Pinterest video pin** · awareness · 1000×1500 · 12.9s · structure: **oneScreen**

> Angle: "Close the month and start the next one" — `tasks[4].label`

## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 6–15s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | Monthly Money Reset: free, motif "ledger", calm personality, cool accent #214b3e | From its definition and Shop listing. 1 real screens and live components (safeToSpendCard, nextActionCard) to show. |
| **Audience** | You want one clear number for what's actually safe to spend, not a full budget system. / You have a mix of bills, some paid automatically and some not, that you'd rather not accidentally spend past. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | One screen, closely: Stay with a single real screen and read it slowly, the way someone planning would. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 3, goal fit 2, already used on Pinterest video pin ×2). Passed over: In their words (its real copy needs 17s to read); How it works (its real copy needs 16s to read), too long for 15s. Other candidates: In their words (7.7), How it works (2.9). |
| **Angle** | "Close the month and start the next one" | A slow read of "Start": Progress this month: bills handled, check-ins, the reserve kept, and spending against your own guide. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "ledger" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "ledger": a ledger is ruled lines, so scenes are revealed row by row. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → accent → light → ink | Grounds light → accent → light → ink: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 12.9s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 6–15s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `ledger`, transitions `lineWipe` → `blurDissolve` → `lineWipe`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
