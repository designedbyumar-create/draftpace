## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 6–15s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | Homeschooling Companion: $34 (list $69), motif "register", calm personality, cool accent #6a4a72 | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You already have a curriculum you chose on purpose and you want something that follows it rather than replacing it. / You teach more than one child and keeping their records separate in your head has stopped working. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | One screen, closely: Stay with a single real screen and read it slowly, the way someone planning would. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 3, goal fit 2, already used on Pinterest video pin ×3). Passed over: In their words (its real copy needs 19s to read); How it works (its real copy needs 15s to read), too long for 15s. Other candidates: In their words (7.6), Is this you? (1.8). |
| **Angle** | "Check whether a topic stuck" | A slow read of "What has happened.": The record: sessions across subjects, a printable homeschool record, and dated notes of what was done. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "register" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "register": a register is filled line by line, so scenes are written in from the top. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → light → ink → accent | Grounds light → light → ink → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Variation** | Rotated the ground rhythm | Its shape was 0.82 similar to travel-companion--pinterest-video--awareness; a different film, not the same one re-coloured. |
| **Variation** | Changed the camera | Its shape was 0.82 similar to personal-finance-companion--pinterest-video--awareness; a different film, not the same one re-coloured. |
| **Variation** | Reordered the transitions | Its shape was 0.74 similar to personal-finance-companion--pinterest-video--awareness; a different film, not the same one re-coloured. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 11.9s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 6–15s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `dolly`, motif `register`, transitions `lineWipe` → `slideUp` → `lineWipe`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
