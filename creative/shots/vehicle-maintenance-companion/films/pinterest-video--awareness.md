## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 6–15s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | Vehicle Maintenance Companion: $34 (list $69), motif "gauge", calm personality, warm accent #b45309 | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You own more than one vehicle, or a vehicle with a maintenance history you already know by heart but have never written down anywhere. / You have bought a used car or inherited one, and its real service history is genuinely unknown to you. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | How it works: Three real steps, each on the real screen where it happens. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 2, goal fit 0, already used on Pinterest video pin ×1). Passed over: In their words (its real copy needs 17s to read), too long for 15s. Other candidates: In their words (7.6), What it is not (-0.6). |
| **Angle** | "How it works" | The listing's own how-it-works, shortest three steps in their real order. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "gauge" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "gauge": a gauge sweeps, so scenes are revealed by a radial sweep. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → ink → light → accent | Grounds light → ink → light → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Edit** | Re-planned with 9 fewer words per line | The fullest real copy could not be read inside 15s on Pinterest video pin. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 12.8s, 3 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 6–15s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `locked`, motif `gauge`, transitions `radial` → `zoomThrough` → `radial`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
