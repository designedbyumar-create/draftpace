## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Instagram Reel, 1080×1920, 12–26s, hook by 1.2s, sound on | Full-screen and swipeable: the first second decides whether anyone stays. Mostly watched with sound, but captions still carry every point. The right edge holds like/comment buttons and the bottom holds the caption, so text lives in the middle band. |
| **Product** | Vehicle Maintenance Companion: $34 (list $69), motif "gauge", calm personality, warm accent #b45309 | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You own more than one vehicle, or a vehicle with a maintenance history you already know by heart but have never written down anywhere. / You have bought a used car or inherited one, and its real service history is genuinely unknown to you. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | What you get: Open on the product, then show what is in it, then the price. | Best fit for a conversion film on Instagram Reel (platform fit 2, goal fit 3, already used on Instagram Reel ×1). Other candidates: In their words (1.6), How it works (1.5). |
| **Angle** | "What you get" | A conversion film for people who already know the problem: product first, contents, price. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "gauge" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "gauge": a gauge sweeps, so scenes are revealed by a radial sweep. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → light → accent → light | Grounds light → light → accent → light: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Sound** | Full sound design, mid-energy bed | Sound-on placement: every transition, landing and reveal has a cue, and the bed ducks under the brand moment. |
| **Length** | 16.5s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Instagram Reel (0.26s a word), inside 12–26s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `gauge`, transitions `radial` → `zoomThrough` → `radial`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
