## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Facebook Reel, 1080×1920, 12–26s, hook by 1.5s, sound off | An older, sound-off-leaning audience: every point on screen, held long enough to read, plainly worded. |
| **Product** | Vehicle Maintenance Companion: $34 (list $69), motif "gauge", calm personality, warm accent #b45309 | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You own more than one vehicle, or a vehicle with a maintenance history you already know by heart but have never written down anywhere. / You have bought a used car or inherited one, and its real service history is genuinely unknown to you. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | Problem cascade: Open on one real problem, let the related worries pile up, then cut to the product holding it. | Best fit for a conversion film on Facebook Reel (platform fit 2, goal fit 2, already used on Facebook Reel ×2). Other candidates: One screen, closely (-1.4), In their words (-1.8). |
| **Angle** | "Registration, insurance and the inspection all come round on different days." | Problem 5 of the listing, "Registration, insurance and the inspection all come round on different days.", answered by its own solution. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "gauge" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "gauge": a gauge sweeps, so scenes are revealed by a radial sweep. |
| **Camera** | Camera "dolly" | Camera "dolly": constant forward movement for a high-energy feed. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → ink → light → accent | Grounds light → ink → light → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, mid-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 19.0s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Facebook Reel (0.3s a word), inside 12–26s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `dolly`, motif `gauge`, transitions `radial` → `zoomThrough` → `radial`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
