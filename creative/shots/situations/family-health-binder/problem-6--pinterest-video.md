## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 10–32s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | Family Health Binder: $34 (list $69), motif "card", calm personality, warm accent #b23a5b | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Situation** | "Not everything belongs on a page you hand to a stranger." | One real moment from Family Health Binder's listing (problemsSolved[5].problem). The film is about this moment and how the product helps with it: "A private choice on every allergy, medication, symptom, vaccine and visit. Each printed page says how many records it left off." |
| **Guide** | "What to bring to a new doctor appointment" (draftpace.com/guides/new-doctor-intake-what-to-bring) | The guide that covers this moment: what to do comes from it, and the film ends on it. |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You are the one who fills in every form, and everyone's allergies, medications and insurance number live only in your head. / You have more than one child, or a child and an aging parent, whose facts you are both responsible for. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | What to do first: The moment, the guide's own checklist for it, then the product that keeps track of it. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 3, goal fit 3, already used for this product ×3). Other candidates: Inside the tool (-17.7), The moment (-22.2). |
| **Angle** | "Not everything belongs on a page you hand to a stranger." | The moment "Not everything belongs on a page you hand to a stranger.", the checklist from "What to bring to a new doctor appointment", then how Family Health Binder helps. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "card" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "card": a binder of cards, so scenes slide in like the next card. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → ink → light → accent | Grounds light → ink → light → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Edit** | Trimmed "what-to-do" to 3 items | Fewer items read properly beats more items skimmed. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 31.7s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 10–32s. |
