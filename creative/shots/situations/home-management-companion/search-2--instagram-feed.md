| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Instagram feed video, 1080×1350, 10–30s, hook by 1.2s, sound off | A 4:5 card in the grid of a feed, autoplayed muted. Craft and composition carry it; text is short and sits well inside the frame. |
| **Product** | Home Base: $49 (list $99), motif "tag", calm personality, cool accent #4f7a5c | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Situation** | "I just bought a house and have no idea what needs doing" | One real moment from Home Base's listing (searchedProblems[1].phrase). The film is about this moment and how the product helps with it: "Tap what your home has across twelve areas and it tells you what each one needs and roughly when, before anything becomes a repair." |
| **Guide** | "New house checklist: what to do in the first week" (draftpace.com/guides/first-week-after-buying-a-house) | The guide that covers this moment: what to do comes from it, and the film ends on it. |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You own or rent a home and would rather be told what needs you this week than keep a mental list you know is incomplete. / You want the boring, expensive things caught early: the water heater, the gutters, the outside tap before the first freeze. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | The moment: The moment as it feels, set dark, then the help in the light, then what to do next. | Best fit for a awareness film on Instagram feed video (platform fit 2, goal fit 3, already used on Instagram feed video ×1). Other candidates: In their words (-1.1), Inside the tool (-3.0). |
| **Angle** | "I just bought a house and have no idea what needs doing" | The moment "I just bought a house and have no idea what needs doing", then how Home Base helps, then what the guide "New house checklist: what to do in the first week" says to do. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "tag" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "tag": things in the home are tagged, so scenes rise like a tag being lifted. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → light → ink → accent | Grounds light → light → ink → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 20.2s, 3 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Instagram feed video (0.3s a word), inside 10–30s. |
