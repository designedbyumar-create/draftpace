## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Facebook feed video, 1080×1350, 10–30s, hook by 1.5s, sound off | Scrolled past in a feed with sound off: say who it is for in the first seconds, name the product early, keep every point on screen and readable at a glance. |
| **Product** | Home Base: $49 (list $99), motif "tag", calm personality, cool accent #4f7a5c | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Situation** | "Seasonal work gets remembered a year too late, long after its month has passed." | One real moment from Home Base's listing (problemsSolved[2].problem). The film is about this moment and how the product helps with it: "Seasonal work raised in the month it actually belongs to." |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You own or rent a home and would rather be told what needs you this week than keep a mental list you know is incomplete. / You want the boring, expensive things caught early: the water heater, the gutters, the outside tap before the first freeze. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | The moment: The moment as it feels, set dark, then the help in the light, then what to do next. | Best fit for a awareness film on Facebook feed video (platform fit 2, goal fit 3, already used for this product ×2). Other candidates: Inside the tool (-10.8). |
| **Angle** | "Seasonal work gets remembered a year too late, long after its month has passed." | The moment "Seasonal work gets remembered a year too late, long after its month has passed.", then how Home Base helps. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "tag" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "tag": things in the home are tagged, so scenes rise like a tag being lifted. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds ink → light → light → accent | Grounds ink → light → light → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 15.5s, 3 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Facebook feed video (0.32s a word), inside 10–30s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `tag`, transitions `slideUp` → `cardSlide` → `slideUp`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
