## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 10–32s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | ADHD Life Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "focus", calm personality, warm accent #86586a | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Situation** | "I have brain fog and can't face life admin" | One real moment from ADHD Life Companion's listing (searchedProblems[4].phrase). The film is about this moment and how the product helps with it: "Built for the difficulty rather than the diagnosis, and it never asks why holding a plan in your head is unreliable right now." |
| **Guide** | "Life admin with brain fog: long covid, illness, grief" (draftpace.com/guides/life-admin-with-brain-fog) | The guide that covers this moment: what to do comes from it, and the film ends on it. |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You have ADHD, or executive function that works like it does, and most productivity tools have made that worse rather than better. / You are dealing with brain fog from long covid, a concussion, chronic illness, grief, new parenthood, menopause, or depression, and holding a plan in your head has stopped being reliable. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | In their words: The moment typed the way a person searches it, then what to do about it, then the product doing it. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 3, goal fit 3, already used for this product ×1, already used on Pinterest video pin ×1). Other candidates: The moment (-1.8), Inside the tool (-7.4). |
| **Angle** | "I have brain fog and can't face life admin" | A situation people search for, "I have brain fog and can't face life admin", with the steps from "Life admin with brain fog: long covid, illness, grief", and how ADHD Life Companion helps. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "focus" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "focus": it is about one thing at a time, so scenes open like an iris onto one thing. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds accent → light → light → accent | Grounds accent → light → light → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Edit** | Cut "step-3" | It would run past 32s, the most Pinterest video pin holds attention for. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 28.9s, 5 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 10–32s. |
