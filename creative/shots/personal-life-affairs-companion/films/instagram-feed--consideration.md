## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Instagram feed video, 1080×1350, 8–20s, hook by 1.2s, sound off | A 4:5 card in the grid of a feed, autoplayed muted. Craft and composition carry it; text is short and sits well inside the frame. |
| **Product** | Personal Life Affairs Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "book", calm personality, cool accent #26374f | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You have been meaning to sort this out for a while and have never found a first step small enough to take. / You would rather be asked one question at a time than handed a folder and told to fill it in. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | How it works: Three real steps, each on the real screen where it happens. | Best fit for a consideration film on Instagram feed video (platform fit 2, goal fit 3). Other candidates: In their words (0.6), One screen, closely (-3.5). |
| **Angle** | "How it works" | The listing's own how-it-works, shortest three steps in their real order. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "book" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "book": a handoff book, so scenes turn like pages. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → light → ink → accent | Grounds light → light → ink → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Edit** | Re-planned with 6 fewer words per line | The fullest real copy could not be read inside 20s on Instagram feed video. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 15.6s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Instagram feed video (0.3s a word), inside 8–20s. |
