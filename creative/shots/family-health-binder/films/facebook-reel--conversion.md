## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Facebook Reel, 1080×1920, 12–26s, hook by 1.5s, sound off | An older, sound-off-leaning audience: every point on screen, held long enough to read, plainly worded. |
| **Product** | Family Health Binder: $34 (list $69), motif "card", calm personality, warm accent #b23a5b | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You are the one who fills in every form, and everyone's allergies, medications and insurance number live only in your head. / You have more than one child, or a child and an aging parent, whose facts you are both responsible for. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | Problem cascade: Open on one real problem, let the related worries pile up, then cut to the product holding it. | Best fit for a conversion film on Facebook Reel (platform fit 2, goal fit 2, already used on Facebook Reel ×1). Other candidates: One screen, closely (-1.3), The question before buying (-1.7). |
| **Angle** | "Not everything belongs on a page you hand to a stranger." | Problem 6 of the listing, "Not everything belongs on a page you hand to a stranger.", answered by its own solution. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "card" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "card": a binder of cards, so scenes slide in like the next card. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → ink → light → accent | Grounds light → ink → light → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Variation** | Rotated the ground rhythm | Its shape was 0.71 similar to alongside--facebook-reel--conversion; a different film, not the same one re-coloured. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, mid-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 24.9s, 5 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Facebook Reel (0.3s a word), inside 12–26s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `card`, transitions `cardSlide` → `blurDissolve` → `cardSlide`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
