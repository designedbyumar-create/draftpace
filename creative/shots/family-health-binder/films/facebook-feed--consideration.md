## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Facebook feed video, 1080×1350, 8–20s, hook by 1.5s, sound off | Scrolled past in a feed with sound off: say who it is for in the first seconds, name the product early, keep every point on screen and readable at a glance. |
| **Product** | Family Health Binder: $34 (list $69), motif "card", calm personality, warm accent #b23a5b | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You are the one who fills in every form, and everyone's allergies, medications and insurance number live only in your head. / You have more than one child, or a child and an aging parent, whose facts you are both responsible for. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | Is this you?: Name the audience in the listing's own descriptions, so the right people stop and everyone else scrolls on. | Best fit for a consideration film on Facebook feed video (platform fit 3, goal fit 2). Other candidates: The question before buying (2.6), Problem cascade (2.0). |
| **Angle** | "You have ever stood at an intake desk trying to remember exactly when a symptom started." | Targeting by recognition: the listing's own audience lines, then what the product does for them. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "card" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "card": a binder of cards, so scenes slide in like the next card. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds accent → light → light → accent | Grounds accent → light → light → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Edit** | Re-planned with 3 fewer words per line | The fullest real copy could not be read inside 20s on Facebook feed video. |
| **Edit** | Cut "brand" | It would run past 20s, the most Facebook feed video holds attention for; the end card still names the product. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 19.3s, 3 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Facebook feed video (0.32s a word), inside 8–20s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `locked`, motif `card`, transitions `cardSlide` → `blurDissolve` → `cardSlide`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
