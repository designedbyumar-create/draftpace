# family-health-binder--pinterest-video--awareness

**Pinterest video pin** · awareness · 1000×1500 · 13.9s · structure: **walkthrough**

> Angle: "How it works" — `micro`

## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 6–15s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | Family Health Binder: $34 (list $69), motif "card", calm personality, warm accent #b23a5b | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You are the one who fills in every form, and everyone's allergies, medications and insurance number live only in your head. / You have more than one child, or a child and an aging parent, whose facts you are both responsible for. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | How it works: Three real steps, each on the real screen where it happens. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 2, goal fit 0). Passed over: In their words (its real copy needs 18s to read), too long for 15s. Other candidates: In their words (7.7), The question before buying (0.3). |
| **Angle** | "How it works" | The listing's own how-it-works, shortest three steps in their real order. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "card" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "card": a binder of cards, so scenes slide in like the next card. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds accent → light → light → accent | Grounds accent → light → light → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Edit** | Re-planned with 6 fewer words per line | The fullest real copy could not be read inside 15s on Pinterest video pin. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 13.9s, 3 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 6–15s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `card`, transitions `cardSlide` → `blurDissolve` → `cardSlide`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
