## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 12–30s, hook by 1.2s, sound on | Search-adjacent audience that will watch a slightly longer explanation if it starts strong. Loops by default. |
| **Product** | Family Health Binder: $34 (list $69), motif "card", calm personality, warm accent #b23a5b | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You are the one who fills in every form, and everyone's allergies, medications and insurance number live only in your head. / You have more than one child, or a child and an aging parent, whose facts you are both responsible for. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | What it is not: Earn trust by stating what the product deliberately does not do, then show what it does instead. | Best fit for a consideration film on YouTube Short (platform fit 2, goal fit 2). Other candidates: Problem cascade (3.2), One screen, closely (2.3). |
| **Angle** | "You want reminders about medications or appointments." | A trust film: the listing's own boundaries, then the thing it does instead, then how it treats your data. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "card" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "card": a binder of cards, so scenes slide in like the next card. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds accent → light → light → accent | Grounds accent → light → light → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Sound** | Full sound design, mid-energy bed | Sound-on placement: every transition, landing and reveal has a cue, and the bed ducks under the brand moment. |
| **Length** | 20.1s, 5 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on YouTube Short (0.26s a word), inside 12–30s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `card`, transitions `cardSlide` → `blurDissolve` → `cardSlide`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
