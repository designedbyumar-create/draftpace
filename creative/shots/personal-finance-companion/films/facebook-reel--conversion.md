## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Facebook Reel, 1080×1920, 12–26s, hook by 1.5s, sound off | An older, sound-off-leaning audience: every point on screen, held long enough to read, plainly worded. |
| **Product** | Personal Finance Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "index", calm personality, cool accent #0d3b2e | From its definition and Shop listing. 4 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You have more than one account, bill, or income source and want them tracked in one real place, not five apps. / You want a next step handed to you, not a dashboard you have to interpret yourself every time. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | Before and after: The problem told as the listing tells it, in the dark; then the product, in the light. | Best fit for a conversion film on Facebook Reel (platform fit 2, goal fit 2). Other candidates: Problem cascade (3.9), Is this you? (3.3). |
| **Angle** | "Money information is scattered across bank apps, statements, memory, and habit." | A sentence of the listing's own problem story, then the product answering it. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "index" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "index": an index of tabs, so scenes slide in like the next tab. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds ink → light → accent → accent | Grounds ink → light → accent → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, mid-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 15.1s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Facebook Reel (0.3s a word), inside 12–26s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `index`, transitions `cardSlide` → `wipe` → `cardSlide`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
