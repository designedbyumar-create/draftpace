## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 6–15s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | Personal Finance Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "index", calm personality, cool accent #0d3b2e | From its definition and Shop listing. 4 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You have more than one account, bill, or income source and want them tracked in one real place, not five apps. / You want a next step handed to you, not a dashboard you have to interpret yourself every time. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | One screen, closely: Stay with a single real screen and read it slowly, the way someone planning would. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 3, goal fit 2, already used on Pinterest video pin ×1). Passed over: In their words (its real copy needs 16s to read), too long for 15s. Other candidates: In their words (7.6), The question before buying (3.1). |
| **Angle** | "Bring in a bank statement without retyping it" | A slow read of "Records": What's actually true right now, area by area: accounts, income, bills, subscriptions, transactions, debt. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "index" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "index": an index of tabs, so scenes slide in like the next tab. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → light → ink → accent | Grounds light → light → ink → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Variation** | Rotated the ground rhythm | Its shape was 0.82 similar to travel-companion--pinterest-video--awareness; a different film, not the same one re-coloured. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 13.3s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 6–15s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `locked`, motif `index`, transitions `cardSlide` → `wipe` → `cardSlide`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
