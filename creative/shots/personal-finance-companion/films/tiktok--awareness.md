## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | TikTok, 1080×1920, 10–22s, hook by 0.8s, sound on | Native, fast and plain-spoken; polish that looks like an ad gets skipped. Sound-on. Hook inside a second. The deepest bottom and right overlays of any placement. |
| **Product** | Personal Finance Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "index", calm personality, cool accent #0d3b2e | From its definition and Shop listing. 4 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You have more than one account, bill, or income source and want them tracked in one real place, not five apps. / You want a next step handed to you, not a dashboard you have to interpret yourself every time. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | In their words: Lead with a phrase people actually search, then show the real screen that answers it. | Best fit for a awareness film on TikTok (platform fit 2, goal fit 3). Other candidates: Problem cascade (4.1), Before and after (3.7). |
| **Angle** | "My finances are scattered across too many apps" | A searched phrase, "My finances are scattered across too many apps", and the listing's own answer to it. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "index" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "index": an index of tabs, so scenes slide in like the next tab. TikTok wants native editing, so hard cuts and whips lead. |
| **Camera** | Camera "dolly" | Camera "dolly": constant forward movement for a high-energy feed. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → accent → light → ink | Grounds light → accent → light → ink: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Full sound design, high-energy bed | Sound-on placement: every transition, landing and reveal has a cue, and the bed ducks under the brand moment. |
| **Length** | 15.1s, 5 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on TikTok (0.24s a word), inside 10–22s. |

Treatment: voice `native`, camera `dolly`, motif `index`, transitions `cut` → `whip` → `cardSlide`, music bed at 0.24 (high energy).
