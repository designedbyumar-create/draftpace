## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 18–45s, hook by 1.2s, sound on | Search-adjacent audience that will watch a slightly longer explanation if it starts strong. Loops by default. |
| **Product** | Personal Finance Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "index", calm personality, cool accent #0d3b2e | From its definition and Shop listing. 4 real screens to show. |
| **Guide** | "How to budget for bills that change every month" (draftpace.com/guides/budget-for-variable-bills) | Teaches from the guide first; Personal Finance Companion is the product the guide hands over to. 3 lists and timelines, 5 questions to draw from. |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You have more than one account, bill, or income source and want them tracked in one real place, not five apps. / You want a next step handed to you, not a dashboard you have to interpret yourself every time. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Material** | Allowed real lines up to 4 words longer | Every structure Personal Finance Companion's copy supports at YouTube Short's normal line length is already used by its other films; a longer real line beats telling the same story twice. |
| **Structure** | In order: Walk the guide's own timeline, one marker at a time, then show the product that keeps it. | Best fit for a awareness film on YouTube Short (platform fit 3, goal fit 3, already used for this product ×1, already used on YouTube Short ×4). Other candidates: The checklist (-16.9), The question people ask (-30.5). |
| **Angle** | "How to budget for bills that change every month" | The guide's timeline "The method": 4 of its 6 markers, in order. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "index" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "index": an index of tabs, so scenes slide in like the next tab. |
| **Camera** | Camera "dolly" | Camera "dolly": constant forward movement for a high-energy feed. |
| **Colour** | Grounds ink → light → light → accent | Grounds ink → light → light → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Full sound design, mid-energy bed | Sound-on placement: every transition, landing and reveal has a cue, and the bed ducks under the brand moment. |
| **Length** | 34.3s, 7 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on YouTube Short (0.26s a word), inside 18–45s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `dolly`, motif `index`, transitions `cardSlide` → `wipe` → `cardSlide`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
