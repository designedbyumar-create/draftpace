## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 18–45s, hook by 1.2s, sound on | Search-adjacent audience that will watch a slightly longer explanation if it starts strong. Loops by default. |
| **Product** | Monthly Money Reset: free, motif "ledger", calm personality, cool accent #214b3e | From its definition and Shop listing. 1 real screens and live components (safeToSpendCard, nextActionCard) to show. |
| **Guide** | "You missed a payment. What to do in the next 48 hours" (draftpace.com/guides/you-missed-a-payment-what-to-do-next) | Teaches from the guide first; Monthly Money Reset is the product the guide hands over to. 2 lists and timelines, 5 questions to draw from. |
| **Audience** | You want one clear number for what's actually safe to spend, not a full budget system. / You have a mix of bills, some paid automatically and some not, that you'd rather not accidentally spend past. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | In order: Walk the guide's own timeline, one marker at a time, then show the product that keeps it. | Best fit for a awareness film on YouTube Short (platform fit 3, goal fit 3). Other candidates: The question people ask (5.0), The checklist (-2.7). |
| **Angle** | "You missed a payment. What to do in the next 48 hours" | The guide's timeline "The first 48 hours, in order": 3 of its 6 markers, in order. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "ledger" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "ledger": a ledger is ruled lines, so scenes are revealed row by row. |
| **Camera** | Camera "dolly" | Camera "dolly": constant forward movement for a high-energy feed. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → light → ink → accent | Grounds light → light → ink → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Full sound design, mid-energy bed | Sound-on placement: every transition, landing and reveal has a cue, and the bed ducks under the brand moment. |
| **Length** | 20.4s, 6 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on YouTube Short (0.26s a word), inside 18–45s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `dolly`, motif `ledger`, transitions `lineWipe` → `blurDissolve` → `lineWipe`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
