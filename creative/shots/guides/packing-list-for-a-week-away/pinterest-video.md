## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 12–35s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | Travel Companion: $34 (list $69), motif "timeline", calm personality, cool accent #00626f | From its definition and Shop listing. 4 real screens to show. |
| **Guide** | "Packing list for a week away, by person" (draftpace.com/guides/packing-list-for-a-week-away) | Teaches from the guide first; Travel Companion is the product the guide hands over to. 2 lists and timelines, 5 questions to draw from. |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You are travelling with other people, and you are the one everybody asks what happens next. / Your trip has more than three moving parts: flights, transfers, stays and reservations that were booked around each other. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | The question people ask: One of the guide's own questions, its plain answer, then the product. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 2, goal fit 2, already used on Pinterest video pin ×10). Other candidates: The checklist (-22.9). |
| **Angle** | "Should I do laundry on a one-week trip?" | A question from the guide's FAQ, "Should I do laundry on a one-week trip?", and the first sentence of its answer. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "timeline" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "timeline": a trip is a line through time, so scenes travel left to right along it. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds ink → light → light → accent | Grounds ink → light → light → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Variation** | Rotated the ground rhythm | Its shape was 0.74 similar to guide-home-maintenance-you-skip-that-costs-the-most--youtube-short; a different film, not the same one re-coloured. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 18.0s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 12–35s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `locked`, motif `timeline`, transitions `slideLeft` → `whip` → `slideLeft`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
