## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 18–45s, hook by 1.2s, sound on | Search-adjacent audience that will watch a slightly longer explanation if it starts strong. Loops by default. |
| **Product** | Vehicle Maintenance Companion: $34 (list $69), motif "gauge", calm personality, warm accent #b45309 | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Guide** | "How to track car registration and insurance renewals" (draftpace.com/guides/car-paperwork-dates-organizer) | Teaches from the guide first; Vehicle Maintenance Companion is the product the guide hands over to. 2 lists and timelines, 5 questions to draw from. |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You own more than one vehicle, or a vehicle with a maintenance history you already know by heart but have never written down anywhere. / You have bought a used car or inherited one, and its real service history is genuinely unknown to you. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | In order: Walk the guide's own timeline, one marker at a time, then show the product that keeps it. | Best fit for a awareness film on YouTube Short (platform fit 3, goal fit 3, already used on YouTube Short ×8). Other candidates: The question people ask (-37.2). |
| **Angle** | "How to track car registration and insurance renewals" | The guide's timeline "One renewal, week by week": 4 of its 5 markers, in order. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "gauge" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "gauge": a gauge sweeps, so scenes are revealed by a radial sweep. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → ink → light → accent | Grounds light → ink → light → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Sound** | Full sound design, mid-energy bed | Sound-on placement: every transition, landing and reveal has a cue, and the bed ducks under the brand moment. |
| **Length** | 25.5s, 7 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on YouTube Short (0.26s a word), inside 18–45s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `gauge`, transitions `radial` → `zoomThrough` → `radial`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
