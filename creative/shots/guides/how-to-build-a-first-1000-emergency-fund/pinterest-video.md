## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 12–35s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | Personal Finance Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "index", calm personality, cool accent #0d3b2e | From its definition and Shop listing. 4 real screens to show. |
| **Guide** | "How to build your first $1,000 emergency fund" (draftpace.com/guides/how-to-build-a-first-1000-emergency-fund) | Teaches from the guide first; Personal Finance Companion is the product the guide hands over to. 1 lists and timelines, 5 questions to draw from. |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You have more than one account, bill, or income source and want them tracked in one real place, not five apps. / You want a next step handed to you, not a dashboard you have to interpret yourself every time. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | Do this: Open on the search itself, then the guide's own numbered steps, then the product. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 3, goal fit 3, already used on Pinterest video pin ×1). Other candidates: The question people ask (-7.8). |
| **Angle** | "$1,000 emergency fund" | Search-led: "$1,000 emergency fund", answered by 2 of the guide's numbered steps ("Set it up in five steps"). |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "index" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "index": an index of tabs, so scenes slide in like the next tab. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → accent → light → ink | Grounds light → accent → light → ink: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Variation** | Rotated the ground rhythm | Its shape was 0.71 similar to sit-personal-finance-companion-search-1--instagram-reel; a different film, not the same one re-coloured. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 23.6s, 5 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 12–35s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `index`, transitions `cardSlide` → `wipe` → `cardSlide`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
