## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 12–35s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | Personal Finance Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "index", calm personality, cool accent #0d3b2e | From its definition and Shop listing. 4 real screens to show. |
| **Guide** | "How to save money this month: count real savings" (draftpace.com/guides/how-to-save-money-fast) | Teaches from the guide first; Personal Finance Companion is the product the guide hands over to. 1 lists and timelines, 3 questions to draw from. |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You have more than one account, bill, or income source and want them tracked in one real place, not five apps. / You want a next step handed to you, not a dashboard you have to interpret yourself every time. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Material** | Allowed real lines up to 4 words longer | Every structure Personal Finance Companion's copy supports at Pinterest video pin's normal line length is already used by its other films; a longer real line beats telling the same story twice. |
| **Structure** | The checklist: The guide's checklist on one screen, item by item, then the product that holds it. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 3, goal fit 3, already used for this product ×2, already used on Pinterest video pin ×4). Other candidates: The question people ask (-19.7). |
| **Angle** | "How to save money this month: count real savings" | A checklist from the guide, "Look for cash that can actually change": 4 of its 8 items. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "index" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "index": an index of tabs, so scenes slide in like the next tab. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → light → ink → accent | Grounds light → light → ink → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Edit** | Trimmed "list" to 3 items | Fewer items read properly beats more items skimmed. |
| **Variation** | Rotated the ground rhythm | Its shape was 0.90 similar to guide-you-missed-a-payment-what-to-do-next--youtube-short; a different film, not the same one re-coloured. |
| **Variation** | Changed the camera | Its shape was 0.74 similar to guide-how-long-to-keep-homeschool-records--pinterest-video; a different film, not the same one re-coloured. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 31.4s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 12–35s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `dolly`, motif `index`, transitions `cardSlide` → `wipe` → `cardSlide`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
