## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 12–35s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | Family Health Binder: $34 (list $69), motif "card", calm personality, warm accent #b23a5b | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Guide** | "Symptom notes for a doctor visit: what to record" (draftpace.com/guides/symptom-notes-for-a-doctor-visit) | Teaches from the guide first; Family Health Binder is the product the guide hands over to. 1 lists and timelines, 3 questions to draw from. |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You are the one who fills in every form, and everyone's allergies, medications and insurance number live only in your head. / You have more than one child, or a child and an aging parent, whose facts you are both responsible for. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Material** | Allowed real lines up to 4 words longer | Every structure Family Health Binder's copy supports at Pinterest video pin's normal line length is already used by its other films; a longer real line beats telling the same story twice. |
| **Structure** | The checklist: The guide's checklist on one screen, item by item, then the product that holds it. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 3, goal fit 3, already used on Pinterest video pin ×16). Other candidates: The question people ask (-50.2). |
| **Angle** | "Symptom notes for a doctor visit: what to record" | A checklist from the guide, "Write one useful entry": 4 of its 7 items. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "card" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "card": a binder of cards, so scenes slide in like the next card. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds accent → light → light → accent | Grounds accent → light → light → accent: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Edit** | Trimmed "list" to 3 items | Fewer items read properly beats more items skimmed. |
| **Edit** | Trimmed "list" to 2 items | Fewer items read properly beats more items skimmed. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 30.6s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 12–35s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `locked`, motif `card`, transitions `cardSlide` → `blurDissolve` → `cardSlide`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
