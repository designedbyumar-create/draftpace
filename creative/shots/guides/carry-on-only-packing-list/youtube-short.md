## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 18–45s, hook by 1.2s, sound on | Search-adjacent audience that will watch a slightly longer explanation if it starts strong. Loops by default. |
| **Product** | Travel Companion: $34 (list $69), motif "timeline", calm personality, cool accent #00626f | From its definition and Shop listing. 4 real screens to show. |
| **Guide** | "Carry-on only packing list: how to fit it in one bag" (draftpace.com/guides/carry-on-only-packing-list) | Teaches from the guide first; Travel Companion is the product the guide hands over to. 4 lists and timelines, 5 questions to draw from. |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You are travelling with other people, and you are the one everybody asks what happens next. / Your trip has more than three moving parts: flights, transfers, stays and reservations that were booked around each other. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Material** | Allowed real lines up to 4 words longer | Every structure Travel Companion's copy supports at YouTube Short's normal line length is already used by its other films; a longer real line beats telling the same story twice. |
| **Structure** | In order: Walk the guide's own timeline, one marker at a time, then show the product that keeps it. | Best fit for a awareness film on YouTube Short (platform fit 3, goal fit 3, already used for this product ×1, already used on YouTube Short ×7). Other candidates: The checklist (-29.4), The question people ask (-33.8). |
| **Angle** | "Carry-on only packing list: how to fit it in one bag" | The guide's timeline "The order": 4 of its 5 markers, in order. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "timeline" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "timeline": a trip is a line through time, so scenes travel left to right along it. |
| **Camera** | Camera "dolly" | Camera "dolly": constant forward movement for a high-energy feed. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → accent → light → ink | Grounds light → accent → light → ink: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Variation** | Rotated the ground rhythm | Its shape was 0.94 similar to guide-travel-document-checklist--youtube-short; a different film, not the same one re-coloured. |
| **Sound** | Full sound design, mid-energy bed | Sound-on placement: every transition, landing and reveal has a cue, and the bed ducks under the brand moment. |
| **Length** | 30.1s, 7 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on YouTube Short (0.26s a word), inside 18–45s. |
