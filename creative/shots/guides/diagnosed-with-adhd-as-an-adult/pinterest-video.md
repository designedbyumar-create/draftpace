## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 12–35s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | ADHD Life Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "focus", calm personality, warm accent #86586a | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Guide** | "Diagnosed with ADHD as an adult: the admin that follows" (draftpace.com/guides/diagnosed-with-adhd-as-an-adult) | Teaches from the guide first; ADHD Life Companion is the product the guide hands over to. 2 lists and timelines, 5 questions to draw from. |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You have ADHD, or executive function that works like it does, and most productivity tools have made that worse rather than better. / You are dealing with brain fog from long covid, a concussion, chronic illness, grief, new parenthood, menopause, or depression, and holding a plan in your head has stopped being reliable. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | In order: Walk the guide's own timeline, one marker at a time, then show the product that keeps it. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 2, goal fit 3). Other candidates: The question people ask (-25.9). |
| **Angle** | "Diagnosed with ADHD as an adult: the admin that follows" | The guide's timeline "The first month, in order": 3 of its 5 markers, in order. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "focus" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "focus": it is about one thing at a time, so scenes open like an iris onto one thing. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → light → accent → light | Grounds light → light → accent → light: a warm accent, so light grounds lead and the accent is saved for the turns. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 30.5s, 6 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 12–35s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `locked`, motif `focus`, transitions `iris` → `blurDissolve` → `iris`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
