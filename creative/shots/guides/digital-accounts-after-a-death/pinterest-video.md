## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 12–35s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | Personal Life Affairs Companion: $49 (list $99), motif "book", calm personality, cool accent #26374f | From its definition and Shop listing. 3 real screens to show. |
| **Guide** | "Digital accounts after a death: preserve, then request" (draftpace.com/guides/digital-accounts-after-a-death) | Teaches from the guide first; Personal Life Affairs Companion is the product the guide hands over to. 1 lists and timelines, 3 questions to draw from. |
| **Stage** | Before launch: helpful and relatable only | Nobody knows Draftpace yet, so films show how it helps with real moments. Who it is not for, questions before buying, what you get and the price wait until there are users to sell to. |
| **Audience** | You have been meaning to sort this out for a while and have never found a first step small enough to take. / You would rather be asked one question at a time than handed a folder and told to fill it in. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Material** | Allowed real lines up to 4 words longer | Every structure Personal Life Affairs Companion's copy supports at Pinterest video pin's normal line length is already used by its other films; a longer real line beats telling the same story twice. |
| **Structure** | The checklist: The guide's checklist on one screen, item by item, then the product that holds it. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 3, goal fit 3, already used for this product ×2). Other candidates: The question people ask (-7.6). |
| **Angle** | "Digital accounts after a death: preserve, then request" | A checklist from the guide, "Preserve the starting position": 4 of its 7 items. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "book" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "book": a handoff book, so scenes turn like pages. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds ink → light → light → accent | Grounds ink → light → light → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Edit** | Trimmed "list" to 3 items | Fewer items read properly beats more items skimmed. |
| **Edit** | Trimmed "list" to 2 items | Fewer items read properly beats more items skimmed. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 30.2s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 12–35s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `book`, transitions `pageTurn` → `blurDissolve` → `pageTurn`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
