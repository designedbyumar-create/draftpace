# travel-companion--pinterest-video--awareness

**Pinterest video pin** · awareness · 1000×1500 · 12.5s · structure: **oneScreen**

> Angle: "Start a new trip" — `tasks[0].label`

## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Pinterest video pin, 1000×1500, 6–15s, hook by 1s, sound off | People are planning, not scrolling: idea-led, calm and save-worthy, worded like what they searched for. Muted, looping, slow; a hard sell reads as an ad and loses the save. |
| **Product** | Travel Companion: $34 (list $69), motif "timeline", calm personality, cool accent #00626f | From its definition and Shop listing. 4 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You are travelling with other people, and you are the one everybody asks what happens next. / Your trip has more than three moving parts: flights, transfers, stays and reservations that were booked around each other. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | One screen, closely: Stay with a single real screen and read it slowly, the way someone planning would. | Best fit for a awareness film on Pinterest video pin (platform fit 3, goal fit 2). Passed over: In their words (its real copy needs 16s to read), too long for 15s. Other candidates: In their words (7.5), The question before buying (3.1). |
| **Angle** | "Start a new trip" | A slow read of "Lisbon and Porto": The trip: where you are now, destinations, and every booking with its reference, with Record a change on each. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "timeline" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "timeline": a trip is a line through time, so scenes travel left to right along it. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → light → ink → accent | Grounds light → light → ink → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 12.5s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Pinterest video pin (0.32s a word), inside 6–15s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `locked`, motif `timeline`, transitions `slideLeft` → `whip` → `slideLeft`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
