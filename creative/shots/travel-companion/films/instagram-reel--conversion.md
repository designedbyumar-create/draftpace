## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Instagram Reel, 1080×1920, 12–26s, hook by 1.2s, sound on | Full-screen and swipeable: the first second decides whether anyone stays. Mostly watched with sound, but captions still carry every point. The right edge holds like/comment buttons and the bottom holds the caption, so text lives in the middle band. |
| **Product** | Travel Companion: $34 (list $69), motif "timeline", calm personality, cool accent #00626f | From its definition and Shop listing. 4 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You are travelling with other people, and you are the one everybody asks what happens next. / Your trip has more than three moving parts: flights, transfers, stays and reservations that were booked around each other. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | Problem cascade: Open on one real problem, let the related worries pile up, then cut to the product holding it. | Best fit for a conversion film on Instagram Reel (platform fit 3, goal fit 2). Other candidates: What you get (6.1), What it is not (6.0). |
| **Angle** | "Confirmation numbers live in six different inboxes and a screenshot." | Problem 1 of the listing, "Confirmation numbers live in six different inboxes and a screenshot.", answered by its own solution. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "timeline" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "timeline": a trip is a line through time, so scenes travel left to right along it. |
| **Camera** | Camera "float" | Camera "float": a gentle float that keeps a held frame alive without demanding attention. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → accent → light → ink | Grounds light → accent → light → ink: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Full sound design, mid-energy bed | Sound-on placement: every transition, landing and reveal has a cue, and the bed ducks under the brand moment. |
| **Length** | 18.5s, 5 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Instagram Reel (0.26s a word), inside 12–26s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `float`, motif `timeline`, transitions `slideLeft` → `whip` → `slideLeft`, music bed at 0.2 (mid energy).
