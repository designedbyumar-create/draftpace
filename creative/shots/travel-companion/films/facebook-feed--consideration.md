## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | Facebook feed video, 1080×1350, 8–20s, hook by 1.5s, sound off | Scrolled past in a feed with sound off: say who it is for in the first seconds, name the product early, keep every point on screen and readable at a glance. |
| **Product** | Travel Companion: $34 (list $69), motif "timeline", calm personality, cool accent #00626f | From its definition and Shop listing. 4 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You are travelling with other people, and you are the one everybody asks what happens next. / Your trip has more than three moving parts: flights, transfers, stays and reservations that were booked around each other. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | How it works: Three real steps, each on the real screen where it happens. | Best fit for a consideration film on Facebook feed video (platform fit 2, goal fit 3). Passed over: Is this you? (its real copy needs 22s to read), too long for 20s. Other candidates: Is this you? (6.3), The question before buying (5.1). |
| **Angle** | "How it works" | The listing's own how-it-works, shortest three steps in their real order. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "timeline" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "timeline": a trip is a line through time, so scenes travel left to right along it. |
| **Camera** | Camera "locked" | Camera "locked": still and precise, so a slow, reading audience is never chasing the frame. |
| **Colour** | Grounds light → accent → light → ink | Grounds light → accent → light → ink: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Edit** | Re-planned with 6 fewer words per line | The fullest real copy could not be read inside 20s on Facebook feed video. |
| **Sound** | Light sound design, low-energy bed | Most of this audience never unmutes, so nothing depends on sound; cues are kept quieter and sparser for those who do. |
| **Length** | 16.3s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on Facebook feed video (0.32s a word), inside 8–20s. |

Treatment: voice `editorial`, camera `locked`, motif `timeline`, transitions `slideLeft` → `whip` → `slideLeft`, music bed at 0.16 (low energy).
