## Analysis and decisions

| | Decision | Because |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement** | TikTok, 1080×1920, 10–22s, hook by 0.8s, sound on | Native, fast and plain-spoken; polish that looks like an ad gets skipped. Sound-on. Hook inside a second. The deepest bottom and right overlays of any placement. |
| **Product** | Travel Companion: $34 (list $69), motif "timeline", calm personality, cool accent #00626f | From its definition and Shop listing. 4 real screens to show. |
| **Audience** | You are travelling with other people, and you are the one everybody asks what happens next. / Your trip has more than three moving parts: flights, transfers, stays and reservations that were booked around each other. | The listing's own audience lines; the director writes for them. |
| **Structure** | Before and after: The problem told as the listing tells it, in the dark; then the product, in the light. | Best fit for a awareness film on TikTok (platform fit 3, goal fit 2). Other candidates: In their words (5.7), Is this you? (4.2). |
| **Angle** | "A trip is easy to plan and hard to run." | A sentence of the listing's own problem story, then the product answering it. |
| **Transitions** | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "timeline" | Transitions follow the product's own identity motif, "timeline": a trip is a line through time, so scenes travel left to right along it. TikTok wants native editing, so hard cuts and whips lead. |
| **Camera** | Camera "dolly" | Camera "dolly": constant forward movement for a high-energy feed. |
| **Colour** | Grounds ink → light → accent → accent | Grounds ink → light → accent → accent: a cool accent, so a darker ground is used for contrast at a turn. |
| **Sound** | Full sound design, high-energy bed | Sound-on placement: every transition, landing and reveal has a cue, and the bed ducks under the brand moment. |
| **Length** | 10.5s, 4 scenes | Each scene is held for the time its words take to read on TikTok (0.24s a word), inside 10–22s. |

Treatment: voice `native`, camera `dolly`, motif `timeline`, transitions `cut` → `whip` → `slideLeft`, music bed at 0.24 (high energy).
