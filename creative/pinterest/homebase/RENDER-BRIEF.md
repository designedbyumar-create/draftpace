# Rendering the Home Base pins (brief for pin builders)

The Home Base pins are app-store promos like `../homeschool/reference-mockup.png`, printed-workbook-page mockups, and guide pins that tell a guide's content in pictures. The visual STYLE is locked: it is the style of the 101 finished Homeschooling Companion pins and the 101 Personal Finance Companion pins, now in this product's sage-green home world. LOOK at 8 to 10 of them before drawing: `creative/out/homeschool/pins/` and `creative/out/pfc/pins/` (for example homeschool `055-*` promo, `004-*` printable; pfc `001-*`, `005-*` promos, `003-*`, `023-*` printable mockups, `034-*`, `048-*`, `096-*`, `066-*` guide pins). A pin is one module in `creative/pinterest/homebase/pins/` exporting `pins` (an array of `{ n, id, html }`; `n` is the idea's number in `creative/pinterest/homebase/ideas-101.json`). Read your ideas' records (`headline`, `sub`, `items`, `visual`, `flags`) first: they are the script. Render from `creative/` with `node pinterest/homebase/render-pins.mjs <yourfile> --only 3,7` (give your FULL file name as the filter, for example `b-<group>`; the filter matches by prefix, so name your module so it is not a prefix of another builder's file: use `hb-<group>` with the group letter plus a trailing `.mjs`, and pass `hb-<group>.mjs`). It prints `⚠` when a headline, feature or list overflows, leaves the page or runs into the product line. Then READ each PNG at full size (the Read tool shows images) and fix what you see until you would post it. Do not commit; do not edit the kit, the bank, or other builders' files. Work efficiently: write the module in one go, render, read each PNG once or twice, fix, report.

## The kit (`pinterest/homebase/kit/`, adapted from the other products' kit; report any bug)
- `bank.mjs`: `idea(n)`, `footFor(i)`, `p` (the product), `C` (sage `#4f7a5c`, sageDeep, sageSoft, ink `#1a2420`, paper `#f4f2ec`, paperLight `#fbfaf6`, wash, rule, cream, oak, kraft, ochre `#8a5a11` (only for "needs a look" and safety), butter, slate (water only), amber).
- `assets.mjs`: `bookPage(n)` (1-36, the real pages of the printed 36-page "The Home Survey" at 200 dpi, 1700x2200; LOOK at a page before using it and check its printed number), `screen("history"|"seasons"|"workspace")` (the three real app screens, 390 px wide, with a Next.js dev badge near the bottom to crop out; demo rows), `asset(name)` (anything else under `creative/out/homebase/assets/`, for example captured screens listed in `CAPTURES.md` when ready).
- `phone.mjs`: `phone({ src, y0, y1, width, tilt, x, y, closed })`: a phone showing the strip y0..y1 of a REAL screenshot.
- `promo.mjs`: `promo({ n, screen: { src, y0, y1, width, tilt }, prop, tone: "plum"|"deep"|"berry" (all sage-and-olive greens), wave: 0|1|2, display, highlight, features, headSize, extraCss })`. The idea records do NOT carry `display`/`features`, so you pass them: `display` = the headline cut into 3 or 4 lines using ONLY words of the idea's headline (it throws otherwise), `highlight` = display lines to show in the light colour, `features` = 3 or 4 rows `{ icon, label, text }` where every label/text is a cut of the idea's `items`, `sub` or its source (never invented). The kit's product line shows the real listing price automatically ("$49 once"): never write a price yourself.
- `printable.mjs`: `printable({ n, scene, paper, extraCss, sub, headline, bottomMax })` and `SCENE_TOP` (372): a cream header block with the bank's headline (split at its colon) and sub, a cream footer with the product line; your `scene` fills y 372..1354.
- `paper.mjs`: props in pin coordinates: `desk`, `sheet({ src, x, y, w, tilt, crop })` (a real page; `crop` is fractions of the page), `pencil`, `mug`, `clipboard`, `binderClip`, `books`, `drawing`. Add your own props and scenes in your file (SVG/CSS). Draw them well: flat vector, soft shadows, real colour.
- `icons.mjs`: `icon("house-line", { size, color, weight })` (Phosphor only, kebab or single-word names).
Whole-page designs: `page(p, { body, css, paper })` from `../../v3/kit.mjs` with `idea(n)`, `footFor(i)` (headline at least 56 px, clear of the product line).

## Rules
1. Words. Every word comes from the idea record (headline, sub, items, proof) or is a label that is real on the page or screen being shown. No invented words, numbers, dates, claims or entries. A printed workbook page stays exactly as printed: no ticks, no filled-in rows, no handwriting on it. Real screens are used as captured; never edit or redraw one. Never claim camera scanning, document upload, AI reading of paperwork, reminders beyond the listing, sharing or assigning jobs, more than one property, insurance valuation, or professional safety advice. Flag `example-tag`: show the kit's small "Example" tag (`.example`, set it at 24 px or more) next to any guide example figure. Never use the numbers "148" or "122" (the product's care-job count is inconsistent between guide and listing).
2. Never redraw a page or screen; crop and place the real image. The page or screen is the hero: a printed page at least 55% of the pin width; a phone at least 430 px wide.
3. VARIETY IS THE OWNER'S TOP RULE. No two pins on the same scene, surface, palette or composition. Each builder owns one "scene family" below; all your pins live in that family's world (its surfaces, props, palette) but each of your pins must differ from your others (surface, layout, tilt, crop, colour mood, hero objects). Never use another family's scene.
4. Props go where they cover no text; in a promo put props at the bottom right or in the very bottom strip (y > 1290); in a printable keep props off the page's text.
5. Text you set yourself (labels, callouts) is at least 22 px, high contrast, nothing touching the foot band.
6. Report: the pin numbers and PNG paths, what each shows (scene, palette, page/screen, crop), anything you could not do and why, and every word on a pin that is not verbatim bank text.
