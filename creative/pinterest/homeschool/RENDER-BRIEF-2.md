# Batch 2: guide-linked pins (brief for pin builders)

Read `RENDER-BRIEF.md` first (the kit, the rules, how to render and review). Batch 1 was mockups of real pages and screens. Batch 2 is GUIDE pins: each links to a guide on the site, shows "Free guide" in the product line, and tells the guide's own content in pictures. You build some of its 26 pins as one module `pins/<yourname>.mjs` exporting `pins` (`{ n, id, html }`, `n` = the idea number in `ideas-101.json`).

Look at the finished pins first: `creative/out/homeschool/pins/` (the 26 PNGs of batch 1: `004-*`, `056-*`, `091-*`, `094-*` for scene craft) and, for homeschool-world art built from real data, `pinterest/v3/products/homeschooling-companion.mjs` (a state tile map from the real requirements data, and a cork-board week with an index card; copy what you need into your own file, never edit it) with its renders `out/v3/homeschooling-companion--*.png` (run `node pinterest/v3/render.mjs homeschooling-companion` if they are missing).

## How a guide pin is built
- `printable({ n, scene, paper, extraCss, sub, headline, bottomMax })` from `kit/printable.mjs` works for any scene, not only printed pages: a cream header block (the bank's headline split at its colon into a small title and a large line, plus the bank's sub), your scene filling y 372..1354, and a cream footer with the product line ("Free guide"). `SCENE_TOP` = 372. You may instead build a whole page yourself with `page(p, { body, css, paper })` from `../../v3/kit.mjs`, `idea(n)` and `footFor(i)` from `kit/bank.mjs`, if the pin wants a full-bleed design (for example a map filling the page); then keep the headline at least 56 px and clear of the product line.
- The idea record (`idea(n)`) is the script: `headline`, `sub`, `items` (the real lines shown on the pin: copy them exactly from the bank), `visual` (what is drawn: follow it, improve it), `proof` (where the words come from). Show the items AS the art: steps as numbered index cards on a cork board, a comparison as two physical things side by side, a timeline as a calendar or a week board, a quote as a handwritten card or a page held in a clip, a scenario as a short day-by-day strip, a myth/truth as a crossed-out slip next to a clean card, a checklist as a clipboard with ticks, a question as the question on a card with the short answer under it. Items may be cut to their first clause only if the cut keeps the source's words; never reword.
- Real data (states): `src/lib/homeschoolStateRequirements.ts` (state, level, note, checklist) is the only source of anything said about a state. The tick labels for the four checklist items are the printed record's own labels in `src/products/homeschooling-companion/book.ts`. Always carry the line "Laws change. Confirm with your state before relying on this." on a state pin. Say "requirements" or "record keeping", never "laws", and never claim a state "requires" more than its note says.
- Pins whose bank record carries the flag `example-tag` use a guide's made-up example: show a small "Example" tag (`.example` class from the kit CSS) next to the example.

## Variety (the owner dislikes pins that look alike)
No two of your pins on the same scene or palette. The world is the homeschool kitchen table: oak, ash, kraft, cork, linen, felt, gingham, cream wall; plum, sage, ochre, slate-blue, terracotta; pencils, mugs, books, clips, washi tape, folders, binders, index cards, sticky notes, a child's drawing, plants, window light. Use different layouts: a full map, a calendar/week board, a clothesline of cards, a stack of folders, a clipboard, a wall pinned with notes, a notebook open, two physical halves for comparisons. Text you set yourself is at least 24 px (card text at least 22 px), high contrast. Nothing may touch the foot band or leave the page.

## Rules that stay
Real words only; never redraw a real screen or page (these pins mostly draw their own scenes, so there are none to redraw); no price anywhere in the art; no claims the product doesn't back (no curriculum, grading, reminders, co-op, filing). Props go where they cover no text. Only create your own `pins/<name>.mjs`; do not edit the kit, other pins or the strategy; do not commit. ALWAYS render with your file name as the filter so another builder's unfinished file cannot break yours: `node pinterest/homeschool/render-pins.mjs <yourname> --only 81,90` (from `creative/`). READ every PNG and iterate until you would post it; no ⚠ lines may remain. Report: pin numbers and PNG paths, what each shows, and anything you could not do.
