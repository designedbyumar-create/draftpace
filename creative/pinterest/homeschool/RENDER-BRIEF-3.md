# Batch 3: guide pins (brief for pin builders)

Batch 3 follows the same rules as batch 2: read `RENDER-BRIEF-2.md` and `RENDER-BRIEF.md` first, in full. Look at the finished pins in `creative/out/homeschool/pins/` (all 52 so far) before you draw, and make yours look different from them: the owner dislikes pins that look alike, so pick scenes, materials and layouts that batches 1 and 2 did not use (for example: a clothesline of cards, a drawer of file folders, a pegboard, a notebook open flat, a fridge door with magnets, a window sill, a shelf of binders, a lunch-bag, a jar of pencils, a wall chart, a postal envelope, a bulletin board, a toast-and-tea breakfast table).

Each pin links to a guide (foot "Free guide") unless its bank record links to the product (then the kit's foot shows the price; that is automatic, you add no price). Every word on a pin is the bank's (`idea(n)`), never reworded; items may be cut to a first clause only if the cut keeps the source's words. Keep the `example-tag` flag rule. State caution line wherever a state is named. British spellings in the bank are kept as they are in the source: do not "correct" them, but tell me about any you see.

Output: your own module `pins/b3-<letter>.mjs` exporting `pins` (`{ n, id, html }`). Render with your file name as the filter: `node pinterest/homeschool/render-pins.mjs b3-<letter>` (from `creative/`). Read every PNG, fix overflow, text under 24 px, anything touching the foot band, and ⚠ lines, until you would post it. Do not commit, and do not edit the kit, other pins or the bank. Report pin numbers, PNG paths, what each shows, and anything you could not do.
