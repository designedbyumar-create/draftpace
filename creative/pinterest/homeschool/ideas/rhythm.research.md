# rhythm slice: research notes

Three WebSearch calls. None returned Pinterest pages or pin counts, so there is no real Pinterest data here, only what the open web shows people searching for and publishing.

- Four-day week: free four-day schedule printables exist (Teach Beside Me has a 4-day and a 5-day layout; Moving Beyond the Page sells ready-made four-day schedules by age), but most results are generic Monday-to-Friday planners. This supports leading with "4 day homeschool week schedule" and "how many days a week for each subject". https://teachbesideme.com/printable-homeschool-schedule/ , https://www.beyondthepage.com/curriculum/schedule/age-6-8-traditional-four-day-schedule.pdf , https://www.proverbialhomemaker.com/4-day-homeschooling-schedule.html
- Attendance: searches are dominated by per-state "attendance log" pages and printable trackers using P/A/S/H/T codes, and by the question "what counts as a school day" (field trips, half days, travel days). This supports the "what counts" comparison and the "lightest record" checklist as queries ("homeschool attendance record", "what counts as a homeschool day"). https://homeschoolpicks.com/how-to-homeschool/homeschool-attendance-records-templates-tips/ , https://templacity.com/wp-content/uploads/free/homeschool-attendance/Homeschool-Attendance-Tracker-A4.pdf
- Checking learning: parents search "how to know if my homeschooler is learning" and are answered with narration, oral questions, "explain it in their own words", and informal weekly check-ins. Our wait-a-week and explain-it angles match that language; "four questions" and "not enough to say" are what we have that the results do not. https://blog.bjupress.com/blog/2016/10/04/5-ways-to-see-if-your-child-is-learning/ , https://mamateaches.com/ways-to-measure-progess-homeschool/ , https://rockyourhomeschool.net/homeschool-assessment/

Grounding note: every word on the pins comes from our guides or The Homeschool Year; the research above only chose queries and angles. The state-specific attendance details in the search results (day counts, hours) were not used.
