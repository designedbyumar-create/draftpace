Treat this as angle and query guidance only; every word on the pins comes from our own guides and printed book.

Our "quit books stay on the list" position is therefore a differentiated angle, not a crowded one. https://www.kuraplan.com/homeschool/templates/reading-log , https://homeschoolhelperonline.com/reading-list/
