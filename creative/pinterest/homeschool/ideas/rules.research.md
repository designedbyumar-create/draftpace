# rules slice: research notes

- Web search returned no Pinterest data (no pin counts, no board names). Results for "just pulled my kids out of school, how to start homeschooling" were general beginner guides (K12 learning store, Well-Trained Mind, International Schooling), all with the same shape: check your state's rules first, handle paperwork, do not buy a curriculum yet, start small. That supports leading the start pins with the order of steps and "curriculum can wait". Source: https://welltrainedmind.com/articles-videos/getting-started and https://internationalschooling.org/blog/new-to-homeschool-here-are-5-steps-to-get-you-started/
- Search for notice of intent and day/hour counts surfaced mostly district forms and template sites; the recurring parent confusion is that days/hours are a separate question from the notice, and that some states list them on the form, some only in reports, some not at all. That supports separate pins for "notice" and "count" rather than one blended pin. Sources: https://www.confessionsofahomeschooler.com/blog/2012/06/notice-of-intent-to-homeschool-form.html/comment-page-1 and https://responsiblehomeschooling.org/?p=10219 (blog and comment pages, so used only to choose angles and queries, never for facts).
- Queries were chosen as the phrases parents type ("just pulled my kids out of school what now homeschool", "homeschool notice of intent checklist", "how long should deschooling last homeschool"). All facts on the pins come from our own guides.
