# product slice: research notes

Two WebSearch calls. Neither returned any real Pinterest data (pin counts, saves, trending queries); the tool does not reach Pinterest. What it did show is what the competing homeschool record-keeping products lead with, so it only informs the angle and the query.

- Competing printable bundles and apps sell per-child records, attendance, a curriculum tracker, and state-specific compliance; spreadsheets "get messy past a couple of kids"; sellers add "does not guarantee compliance with any state" lines. That supports leading app promos with multiple children, a printed record, and the state card (with its "Laws change" caution), and keeping every state line to the file's own note.
- Printable pages are the main thing homeschool parents get from planner pages (weekly, daily log, subject, reading log, attendance, sized for a binder), so the printable mockups use real book pages and name them as the search phrase ("homeschool weekly planner printable", "homeschool daily log printable").
