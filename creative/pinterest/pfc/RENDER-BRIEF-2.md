# Rendering the rest of the Personal Finance Companion pins (brief for pin builders)

Read `RENDER-BRIEF.md` first, in full (the kit, the rules, how to render and review). It describes batch 1, which is finished: look at its 26 PNGs in `creative/out/pfc/pins/` (ideas 1 5 9 3 7 11 15 23 29 68 2 26 18 10 50 48 101 100 66 58 4 81 69 51 80 34) and at several of the 101 Homeschooling Companion pins in `creative/out/homeschool/pins/` so you know the locked visual style. You build the other 75 pins, in 15 groups. Your module is `pins/b2-<group>.mjs` exporting `pins` (`{ n, id, html }`, `n` = the idea number in `ideas-101.json`). Render with `node pinterest/pfc/render-pins.mjs b2-<group> --only <your ideas>` (always filter by your own file name). READ every PNG at full size; iterate until you would post it; no ⚠ lines. Do not commit; do not edit the kit, the bank or other files.

## Variety (the owner's top rule)
Each group owns ONE scene family (below). All your pins live in that family's world (its surfaces, props and palette), but each of your pins must still look different from your others: different surface, layout, tilt, crop and colour mood, and different hero objects. Never use another group's family, and never the batch 1 scenes: desks with blotters, binders, clipboards on trays, marble, laptop decks, kitchen counters with fruit bowls, tiles, butcher-block, lock-screen and hall-console mail scenes, wall calendars and year planners, snowy windows, clotheslines of cards, glass coin jars, till-roll receipts, graph-paper desks, ledger, baize, passbooks, gingham, terrazzo.

Scene families: G1 garden potting bench (seed packets, trowel, terracotta pots, twine, wooden crates, watering can); G2 bakery counter (flour, a kitchen scale, cooling racks, bread paper bags, a recipe card stand); G3 garage workbench (a toolbox, car keys, jars of screws, oil-cloth, a pegboard of tools, a spirit level); G4 café table (a newspaper, cups and saucers, pastries, a menu card, a small vase); G5 stationery shop (pencil cases, rulers, erasers, washi tape, notebooks stacked, index tabs); G6 hardware store (paint swatches, a tape measure, small drawers with brass labels, sandpaper, a spirit level on a bench); G7 sewing and craft table (thread spools, buttons, fabric swatches, scissors, a pin cushion); G8 library corner (a bookshelf end, a reading lamp, a stack of cloth books, a ladder, bookmarks); G9 entryway (coat hooks, a coat, an umbrella stand, a shoe rack, a key tray on a bench); G10 moving day (cardboard boxes, packing tape, a marker, labels, bubble wrap); G11 night study (a dark desk, a green lamp, headphones, a laptop's edge, a notepad); G12 suitcase and travel desk (a suitcase, luggage tags, a map, a passport-free wallet, a ticket envelope); G13 drafting table (rulers, set squares, tracing paper, compasses, a pencil tray); G14 art studio (a paint palette, brushes in jars, canvases, a stool, rolled paper); G15 app promos (`promo()` layouts).

## Words and honesty
As in `RENDER-BRIEF.md` rules 1 to 6: every word from the idea record (headline, sub, items, proof) or real on the page/screen shown; never redraw a real page or screen; never claim bank linking, cancelling for you, net worth calculation, investments, tax, or advice; the `example-tag` flag means the kit's small "Example" tag (24 px or more) next to any guide example figure; the product line shows the listing price automatically on product pins and "Free guide" on guide pins; no price anywhere else. Text you set is at least 22 px.

## Printed workbook pages
`bookPage(n)` (1-81). LOOK at a page before you use it; its printed page number can differ from the PDF page. Pages already used in batch 1: 10, 13, 26, 27, 38, 42, 54, 55, 79 (and fan pages 56 to 76): if your idea needs one of those, crop or compose it very differently (a tight crop of one table, a different tilt and scene) so it does not look like the batch 1 pin. Leave printed pages blank as printed; props (a pencil, a highlighter) go beside them, never marks on the page.

Real screens captured from the real components with demo data are in `creative/out/pfc/assets/` (the capture agent is producing them; the file names are in `creative/out/pfc/assets/CAPTURES.md` when ready).
