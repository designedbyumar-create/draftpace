# Rendering the Personal Finance Companion pins (brief for pin builders)

Batch 1 of the PFC pins is 26 pins: app-store promos like `../homeschool/reference-mockup.png`, printed-workbook-page mockups, and guide pins that tell a guide's content in pictures. The visual STYLE is locked: it is the style of the 101 finished Homeschooling Companion pins, in this product's forest green and finance world. LOOK at 8 to 10 of them before drawing: `creative/out/homeschool/pins/` (for example `055-*` app promo, `004-*` printable on a clipboard, `091-*`, `062-*`, `043-*`, `048-*`, `100-*`). A pin is one module in `creative/pinterest/pfc/pins/` exporting `pins` (an array of `{ n, id, html }`; `n` is the idea's number in `creative/pinterest/pfc/ideas-101.json`). Read your ideas' records (`headline`, `sub`, `items`, `visual`, `flags`) first: they are the script. Render from `creative/` with `node pinterest/pfc/render-pins.mjs <yourfile> --only 3,7` (ALWAYS give your file name as the filter so another builder's unfinished file cannot break yours). It prints `⚠` when a headline, feature or list overflows, leaves the page or runs into the product line. Then READ each PNG at full size (the Read tool shows images) and fix what you see until you would post it. Do not commit; do not edit the kit, the bank, or other builders' files.

## The kit (`pinterest/pfc/kit/`, a copy of the homeschool kit adapted to this product; report any bug)
- `bank.mjs`: `idea(n)`, `footFor(i)`, `p` (the product), `C` (forest, forestDeep, forestSoft, ink, paper `#fafbf9`, wash `#eef4f1`, rule `#c8d6cd`, cream, oak, kraft, butter, pink, amber).
- `assets.mjs`: `bookPage(n)` (1-81, the real pages of the 81-page Printable Finance Companion workbook at 200 dpi, 1700x2200: pages are the ones the idea's `visual` names; LOOK at a page before using it, and check its printed number against the PDF page), `screen("bills"|"debt"|"printables"|"records")` (the four real app screens, 390 px wide, with a Next.js dev badge near the bottom to crop out; demo rows), `asset(name)`.
- `phone.mjs`: `phone({ src, y0, y1, width, tilt, x, y, closed })`: a phone showing the strip y0..y1 of a REAL screenshot.
- `promo.mjs`: `promo({ n, screen: { src, y0, y1, width, tilt }, prop, tone: "plum"|"deep"|"berry" (all forest greens), wave: 0|1|2, display, highlight, features, headSize, extraCss })`. The idea records do NOT carry `display`/`features`, so you pass them: `display` = the headline cut into 3 or 4 lines using ONLY words of the idea's headline (it throws otherwise), `highlight` = the display lines to show in the light colour, `features` = 3 or 4 rows `{ icon, label, text }` where every label/text is a cut of the idea's `items`, `sub` or its source (never invented). The kit's product line shows the real listing price automatically ("$49 once"): never write a price yourself.
- `printable.mjs`: `printable({ n, scene, paper, extraCss, sub, headline, bottomMax })` and `SCENE_TOP` (372): a cream header block with the bank's headline (split at its colon) and sub, a cream footer with the product line; your `scene` fills y 372..1354.
- `paper.mjs`: props in pin coordinates: `desk`, `sheet({ src, x, y, w, tilt, crop })` (a real page; `crop` is fractions of the page), `pencil`, `mug`, `clipboard`, `binderClip`, `books`, `drawing`. Add your own props and scenes in your file (SVG/CSS). Draw them well: flat vector, soft shadows, real colour.
- `icons.mjs`: `icon("calendar-check", { size, color, weight })` (Phosphor only, kebab or single-word names).
Whole-page designs: `page(p, { body, css, paper })` from `../../v3/kit.mjs` with `idea(n)`, `footFor(i)` (headline at least 56 px, clear of the product line).

## Rules
1. Words. Every word comes from the idea record (headline, sub, items, features, proof) or is a label that is real on the page or screen being shown. No invented words, numbers, dates, claims or entries. A printed workbook page stays exactly as printed: no ticks, no filled-in rows, no handwriting on it (a blank page stays blank). Real screens are used as captured; never edit or redraw one. Never claim bank linking, cancelling for you, net worth calculation, investments, or anything the idea does not say. Flag `example-tag`: show the kit's small "Example" tag (`.example`, set it at 24 px or more) next to any guide example figure. Pins that refer to your state's rules or to money decisions carry no advice claims.
2. Never redraw a page or screen; crop and place the real image. The page or screen is the hero: a printed page at least 55% of the pin width; a phone at least 430 px wide.
3. VARIETY IS THE OWNER'S TOP RULE. No two pins on the same scene, surface, palette or composition. In this batch each builder owns a "scene family" below, so your pins differ from the other builders' by construction; inside your family make every pin different from your other pins too. Never use a scene from the other families, and avoid the homeschool pins' exact scenes where a finance prop fits (use binders, folders, receipts, a calculator, jars, coins in a glass, envelopes, bank cards, sticky notes, a phone, index tabs, a wall calendar, a ruler, a mug).
4. Props go where they cover no text: in an app promo put props at the bottom right or in the very bottom strip (y > 1290); in a printable keep props off the page's text.
5. Text you set yourself (labels, callouts) is at least 22 px (card text at least 22 px), high contrast, nothing touching the foot band.
6. Report: the pin numbers and PNG paths, what each shows (scene, palette, page/screen, crop), anything you could not do and why, and the words on the pin that are not verbatim bank text.

## Scene families (batch 1)
- Builder A, app promos (3 pins): `promo()` layouts only. Vary tone, wave, tilt direction, crop, prop (calculator, mug, coin, receipt roll, a closed binder, a pen).
- Builder B, workbook pages on desks (5 pins): desk, binder and clipboard worlds: a walnut desk with a leather blotter, a white desk with a laptop edge, a ring binder open on linen, a clipboard on a wooden tray, a marble slab. Real pages via `sheet()` or your own placement of `bookPage()`.
- Builder C, workbook and template pages in a home/kitchen world (4 pins): a kitchen counter with a fruit bowl, ceramic tiles, a window ledge, a breakfast table, a shelf. Real pages and the guide's own template shown as designed.
- Builder D, the phone and mail world (4 pins): a lock screen / phone on a table, a hall console with mail and a key bowl, a pile of envelopes and receipts, a wallet and bank cards, a letter-opener.
- Builder E, the calendar, year-end and holiday world (5 pins): a wall calendar, a year planner on a wall, wrapping paper and gift tags, a snowy window, a December desk with a candle, a clothesline of cards. No Christmas cliché overload: finance first.
- Builder F, the jars, money and numbers world (5 pins): glass jars of coins, a piggy-less savings jar, a pocket calculator, a till roll, a paper bag of groceries with a receipt, sticky notes on a fridge-free wall, a ledger book, a coin stack as a bar chart.
