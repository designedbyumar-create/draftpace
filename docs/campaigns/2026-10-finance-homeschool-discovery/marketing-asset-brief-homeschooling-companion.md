## Four short-video concepts (15-30s each, vertical)

1. **"Tap it done."** Just the real Today screen: a subject circle tapped, "Did not get to it" showing honestly for the unfinished one, two children's lists side by side. Caption: "Each kid, kept separate. One tap a day." Screen-recorded directly from the live product, not a mockup.
2. **"Not a curriculum."** Text-on-screen format, no footage needed: "This does not tell you what to teach." / "It has no lessons. No scheme of work." / "It just remembers what you actually did." Aimed squarely at the audience exclusion the product states outright, and the real competitive gap against curriculum-selling tools researched in `README.md` Section 3.
3. **"The state check."** A state name being set once, then the real printed-record state-requirements list appearing beside what's recorded. Caption: "Know what your state actually asks for, before someone else asks you." Directly answers the most-searched real pain point sourced in the listing's own `searchedProblems`.
4. **"No scores, ever."** A check result screen showing a per-topic honest result, including one that says "not enough to say yet." Caption: "Never a score. Never compared to anyone else's pace." This is the single sharpest point of difference against Homeschool Planet and similar grade-focused competitors identified in research, worth its own asset rather than folding into the walkthrough.
