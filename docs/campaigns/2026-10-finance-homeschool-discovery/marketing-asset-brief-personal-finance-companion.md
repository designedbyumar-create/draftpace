## 60-second walkthrough script

**Hook (0:00-0:06)** On screen: a phone showing a generic bank balance. VO: "Your balance isn't what's actually yours to spend." Cut to the real product's "Available to spend" banner.

**Problem (0:06-0:16)** VO: "Money's scattered across bank apps, bills you half-remember, and a spreadsheet that's always a week out of date." Screen: quick cuts of the product's own real seven areas listed in Settings (accounts, income, bills, subscriptions, transactions, debt, savings) being added one at a time, per the product's real onboarding flow ("the Companion asks about one area at a time").

**Solution (0:16-0:40)** VO: "Personal Finance Companion adds it all up into one Available Money figure, with the full breakdown one tap away." Screen: the real "How Draftpace got this" breakdown opening. VO continues: "See exactly when your debt is gone." Screen: the real payoff-plan screen, screen-recorded from the live app, the extra-payment slider moving, the debt-free month updating live. VO: "Tick a bill paid, and what's left updates itself." Screen: a bill being ticked in Bills.

**Differentiator (0:40-0:52)** VO: "No bank login, ever. You enter your own numbers, so nothing here reads your real account credentials." Screen: the product's own FAQ line on screen as on-screen text. VO: "One real Attention inbox, only genuine gaps, never a fake task to pad a list."

**CTA (0:52-0:60)** VO: "One payment. Yours for life. Personal Finance Companion." Screen: price card, $49 (was $99), "Get Lifetime Access" button.

## Four short-video concepts (15-30s each, vertical)

1. **"The slider."** Just the payoff-plan screen: tap, drag the extra-payment slider, watch the debt-free date jump from 2031 to 2030. No voiceover needed beyond on-screen captions ("Pay $100 extra a month" -> "Debt-free 14 months sooner"). Ends on the price card. Screen-recorded directly from the live product (a real or sample account), not a mockup.
2. **"One number."** Open on a cluttered desk of real bank apps/statements (stock or staged footage), cut hard to the product's "Available to spend" figure. Caption: "Not your balance. What's actually yours." CTA card at the end.
3. **"The bill tick."** A single bill getting marked paid, and the "what's left to pay" line updating in real time. Caption: "No more wondering if you already paid this." Shortest, most literal feature demo, good for retargeting people who already visited the product page.
4. **"No bank login."** Split screen: a big-name finance app's "Connect your bank" screen (described, not shown, to avoid using a competitor's trademarked UI) on one side, Personal Finance Companion's manual entry on the other. Caption: "We never ask for your bank password. You type the numbers." Trust-angle video, aimed at the privacy-conscious segment the research in `README.md` Section 3 identified as a real, distinct audience from the big three.
