## Research leads checked in the search tool

- FTC used-car guidance: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-used-car-dealer . A history report is not a substitute for an independent inspection. Confirm source text before final copy.
- FTC overview: https://consumer.ftc.gov/features/feature-0040-used-cars . History information may include repair events, but is not a comprehensive maintenance record.
- NHTSA recalls: https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls . Use the VIN lookup and explicitly state its scope/limitations after opening the official page. Do not imply a clear recall result certifies the car safe.
- Reddit language: https://www.reddit.com/r/cars/comments/1db66c5/ . Anecdotes describe service events missing from reports and difficulty retrieving past work. This is pain-point language, not authority for repair decisions.

These are search-result leads, not a substitute for opening and verifying every consequential claim. No exact search-volume figures are available. Quora access remains blocked in the original research run.

## Required answer structure and tools

1. Separate service records, a vehicle-history report, a recall lookup, and a current mechanical inspection. Four different questions; none replaces the others.
2. Recover records: seller, supplying dealer or previous service locations, manufacturer account/portal where available, receipts and digital documents. Respect privacy and do not promise access to another owner's personal data.
3. Record what is documented, what is reported verbally, and what is unknown. Never silently turn unknown into never done.
4. Model/year/engine-specific manual and manufacturer schedule. No invented universal oil, belt, chain, transmission-fluid or battery interval.
5. Mechanic conversation with authorization boundaries, written itemised priorities and evidence for recommendations. Avoid diagnosing safety from internet descriptions.
6. Distinct visual tools: records-recovery path, three evidence states, service-baseline sheet, and a mechanic question script. At least two genuinely relevant annotated images plus accessible HTML equivalents.
7. Free baseline worksheet and optional Vehicle Maintenance Companion handoff after substantive help. Verify actual product features from the local registry; no booking, recall or diagnostic claims unless implemented.

## Fact-check risks

- Unknown service history is not proof of neglect.
- A full-looking report is not proof all maintenance happened.
- Replacing everything is not a defensible default.
- A visual check cannot establish timing-belt age or every hidden condition.
- A recall lookup is not a complete mechanical condition report.
- Do not publish generic "safe to drive" or "keep driving" advice from checklist answers.
- Distinguish the VIN/manufacturer records path for US readers from procedures elsewhere.
