## What this proves

Draftpace is receiving a small number of impressions for practical, problem-shaped queries. The strongest early signals are doctor-visit preparation, four-day homeschooling, used-car records, homeschool paperwork, car maintenance, travel disruption, and hotel booking problems. Several pages already appear around positions 5–12 but receive no clicks, which makes the first priority search-result promise, exact intent match, and page usefulness rather than publishing another unrelated topic.

It does **not** prove monthly search volume, that the site is permanently capped at this traffic level, or that every non-impression page has no demand. Monthly volume needs a keyword dataset such as Google Ads Keyword Planner or another source with explicit volume methodology.

## Coverage context

The supplied coverage chart reaches **148 indexed** and **50 not indexed** at its latest dated row, 9 November 2026. The issue export also lists a redirect error, one noindex issue, one robots issue, and 40 discovered-but-currently-not-indexed pages. Those technical/indexing issues matter, but the content audit treats them separately from whether an article satisfies its reader.
