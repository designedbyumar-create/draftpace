# Draftpace article audit summary

Generated 17 November 2026 from the live crawl of 137 `/guides/` articles, the stored per-article web/Reddit/Quora research, and the supplied Search Console export.

## What is wrong across the library

- The articles are mostly written as explanatory pages, while the searcher often needs a usable checklist, tracker, schedule, script, or decision rule immediately.
- High-stakes pages need stronger claim-level primary sourcing and jurisdiction/update ownership. One UK death-registration article contains a confirmed timing error; see `verified-findings.md`.
- Many articles repeat the answer in the body, examples, tables, and FAQs instead of using the FAQ for new edge cases.
- Visual evidence is not consistently useful as a reading experience. The crawl can identify empty alt text, but it cannot prove whether an image was AI-generated.
- Google Search Console shows very low exposure and no clicks in the supplied period. It does not provide total market volume, so monthly volume remains unknown until a keyword dataset or Ads/third-party source is supplied.
